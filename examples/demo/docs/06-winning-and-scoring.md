# Winning, losing and the archive score

## How the archivists win

The archivists win together as soon as all five collections carry a restoration marker. The game
ends immediately, in the middle of a turn if necessary: no further event card is revealed.

## How the archivists lose

There are two ways to lose. The first is the blight marker reaching space 12, described on the
Ink Blight page. The second is the folio deck running out for the second time, described in the
turn structure.

## The archive score

After a win, take 12 minus the space of the blight marker. That number is your archive score.

- 10 to 12: a legendary restoration, the city will tell stories about it.
- 6 to 9: a solid restoration.
- 0 to 5: a narrow escape.

The archive score is optional. Some groups play a series of three games and add up the scores.

## Tips for a first win

Scrub the wings that hold two blight tokens before the marker reaches space 4. Restore the Ledgers
early, because the East wing is the first one many events hit. And keep the Conservator close to
the wing with the most blight.
