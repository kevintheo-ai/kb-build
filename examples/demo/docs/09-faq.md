# Frequently asked questions

## Can archivists in different wings lend each other cards?

No. Lending works only between archivists in the same wing, or between archivists who both stand
in the Reading Hall. If you need a card from a teammate across the archive, one of you has to walk
first.

## Does lending cost an action for both archivists?

No. Only the active archivist spends an action. The other archivist simply agrees or refuses.

## Can a collection be restored twice?

No. Once a collection carries a restoration marker, it is done for the rest of the game. Any folio
cards you collected for it are better lent to a teammate.

## What happens if an event wants to place blight in a sealed wing?

A sealed wing already holds three tokens. The new token goes to the Reading Hall instead, as
described on the Ink Blight page.

## Is the hand limit checked when someone lends me a card?

No. The hand limit of seven is only checked at the end of your own gather phase.

## May the Binder use the ability twice in one turn?

No. The Binder ability works once per turn, even if the Binder restores two collections in the
same turn.

## Can we look at the next event card?

No. The event deck stays face down. Only Visiting Scholar and similar cards change the next turn,
and they never let you look ahead.
