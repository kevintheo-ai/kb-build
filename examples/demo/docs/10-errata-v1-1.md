# Errata and rule changes in version 1.1

Version 1.1 changes a few rules after the first year of play. Where this page differs from an older
page, this page wins.

## The Scribe's hand limit

The Scribe now has a hand limit of eight cards instead of seven. Groups found that the Scribe's
extra card in the gather phase was often discarded right away.

## The Ledgers

The Ledgers now need two Ink, one Seal and one Thread instead of three Ink and one Seal. Ink cards
were too scarce in games where the Star Charts were restored first.

## The Conservator in sealed wings

The Conservator may search and restore in sealed wings. Other archivists still can only walk out or
scrub there.

## Two-player games

In a two-player game, the blight marker now starts on space 1. Each archivist still starts with
five folio cards.

## Unchanged

Everything else in version 1.0 stays as it is, including the blight spread at spaces 4 and 8 and
the loss at space 12.
