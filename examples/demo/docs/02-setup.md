# Setting up the game

## Board and blight

Unfold the archive board in the middle of the table. Put the blight marker on space 0 of the
blight track. Then place one blight token in each of the four wings: the archive starts damaged
everywhere, but only lightly. Keep the remaining blight tokens in a pile next to the board.

Put the five restoration markers beside the board. They are placed on a collection when the
archivists restore it.

## Folio cards

Shuffle all 72 folio cards and place them face down as the folio deck. Leave room next to it for
a discard pile.

Deal four folio cards to each archivist. Players hold their folio cards in their hand. You may tell
the others which kinds you hold, but you may not show them your cards.

## Two-player games

In a two-player game, each archivist starts with five folio cards instead of four, because two
players have fewer chances to lend each other cards. Everything else in the setup stays the same.

## Roles and pawns

Shuffle the four role cards and deal one to each player, or let everyone choose. Each role gives
its archivist one special ability, described on the roles page. All pawns start in the Reading
Hall.

## Events and first player

Shuffle the 15 archive event cards and put them face down next to the blight track. The player
who most recently visited a library takes the Keeper's bell and plays first. Play then continues
clockwise.

## Harder games

Experienced groups can start the blight marker on space 2 instead of space 0. This removes two
turns of safety and makes every blight spread arrive earlier.
