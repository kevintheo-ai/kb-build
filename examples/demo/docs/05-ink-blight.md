# Ink Blight

## The blight track

The blight track runs from space 0 to space 12. In every archive phase the blight marker moves
forward by the number printed on the revealed event card. Most cards show 1, a few show 2, and the
calm "Quiet Night" card shows 0. The marker never moves back.

## Blight spreads at 4 and 8

When the blight marker reaches or passes space 4, and again when it reaches or passes space 8, the
blight spreads: place one more blight token in every wing that already holds at least one blight
token. Wings without any blight token are not affected, which is why scrubbing a wing clean before
space 4 is worth so much.

## Sealed wings

A wing with three or more blight tokens is sealed. Archivists in a sealed wing can only walk out or
scrub; they cannot search, restore or lend there. As soon as a scrub brings the wing below three
tokens, it opens again.

If a sealed wing would receive a fourth blight token, the token goes to the Reading Hall instead.
The Reading Hall can hold any number of tokens and is never sealed.

## When the blight reaches space 12

If the blight marker reaches space 12, the blight reaches the Founders' Vault and all players lose
at once, even if four of the five collections are already restored. Watch the track: from space 9
on, a single unlucky event card can end the game.

## Running out of blight tokens

If the pile of blight tokens is empty when a token must be placed, move the blight marker forward
by one space for every token that cannot be placed.
