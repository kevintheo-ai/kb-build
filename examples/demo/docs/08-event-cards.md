# Archive event cards

There are 15 archive event cards. The number in the corner is how far the blight marker moves after
the card is resolved.

## Damaging events

- **Leaking Roof** (2): place one blight token in the East wing.
- **Damp Corner** (1): place one blight token in the wing with the most blight tokens.
- **Broken Window** (1): place one blight token in the North wing.
- **Spores in the Air** (2): place one blight token in every wing without blight.
- **Fallen Shelf** (1): the active archivist discards one folio card of their choice.
- **Burst Pipe** (2): place two blight tokens in the South wing.

## Helpful events

- **Quiet Night** (0): nothing happens.
- **Donation** (1): every archivist draws one folio card.
- **Visiting Scholar** (1): the next archivist may take four actions in the work phase.
- **Dry Wind** (1): remove one blight token from any wing.

## Restricting events

- **Lost Key** (1): until the end of the next turn, nobody may lend cards.
- **Closed Stairs** (1): until the end of the next turn, nobody may walk into the West wing.
- **Inspection** (1): the active archivist shows their hand to everyone.
- **Power Cut** (1): searching is not allowed until the end of the next turn.
- **Rival Collector** (1): discard the top three cards of the folio deck.
