# Actions

Each action costs one of your three actions in the work phase.

## Walk

Move your pawn from a wing to the Reading Hall, or from the Reading Hall to any wing. The wings are
not connected to each other directly: to get from the East wing to the West wing you walk through
the Reading Hall, which takes two actions.

## Search

Draw one folio card. You may only search in a wing that has no blight token, or in the Reading
Hall. Blighted shelves are too fragile to search.

## Restore

Discard the set of folio cards that a collection needs, then place a restoration marker on that
collection. You must stand in the wing that holds the collection. The sets are:

- Maps: two Vellum, one Ink and one Seal
- Ledgers: three Ink and one Seal
- Star Charts: two Ink and two Thread
- Songbooks: two Thread, one Vellum and one Ink
- Charters: two Seal and two Vellum

A restored collection stays restored for the rest of the game, even if blight later spreads into
its wing.

## Lend

Give one folio card to another archivist, or take one folio card from another archivist, if the
other archivist agrees. Lending only works when both pawns stand in the same wing or both stand in
the Reading Hall. Only the active archivist spends an action for lending.

## Scrub

Remove one blight token from the wing you stand in and put it back on the pile. Scrubbing does not
move the blight marker back; it only cleans the shelves of your wing.
