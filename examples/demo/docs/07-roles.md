# The four roles

Each archivist has one role. The ability on the role card is always active unless the card says
"once per turn".

## Binder

Once per turn, when restoring, the Binder may use any two folio cards of the same kind as one card
of any other kind. Example: two Thread cards can count as one Seal card for the Charters.

## Cartographer

The Cartographer may walk two steps for one action, for example from the East wing through the
Reading Hall into the West wing. The Cartographer is the fastest way to reach a wing that is about
to be sealed.

## Scribe

The Scribe draws three folio cards instead of two in the gather phase. Because the Scribe draws
more cards, the Scribe often lends cards to the others.

## Conservator

When the Conservator scrubs, two blight tokens are removed with a single action. The Conservator is
the strongest answer to the spreading blight.
