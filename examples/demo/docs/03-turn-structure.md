# Structure of a turn

On your turn you go through three phases in this order: the work phase, the gather phase and
the archive phase. Then the archivist on your left takes the next turn.

## Work phase

Take up to three actions. You may take the same action more than once, and you may also take
fewer than three actions. Unused actions are lost; they cannot be saved for a later turn. The
actions are walk, search, restore, lend and scrub. They are described on the actions page.

## Gather phase

Draw two folio cards from the folio deck. Your hand limit is seven cards: if you hold more than
seven cards at the end of the gather phase, discard down to seven. The hand limit is only checked
at that moment, so you may hold more cards during the other players' turns.

When the folio deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile once to form a new deck. If the deck runs
out a second time, the archive closes for good and all players lose.

## Archive phase

Reveal the top archive event card, read it aloud and resolve it. Then move the blight marker
forward by the number printed in the corner of the card. Put the card on the event discard pile.

If the event deck is empty when you need to reveal a card, shuffle the event discard pile to form
a new event deck. Unlike the folio deck, the event deck never causes a loss.

## A quick example

Mira plays the Cartographer. She walks from the Reading Hall to the North wing, searches there and
lends a Thread card to Jonas, who stands in the North wing too. She then draws two folio cards,
reveals the event card "Quiet Night" and moves the blight marker forward by zero spaces.
