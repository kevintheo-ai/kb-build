# The Archivists of Vellmoor: overview and components

*A fictional cooperative board game, written for the kb-build demo. It does not exist outside this folder.*

## The story

The great archive of Vellmoor flooded during the spring thaw. The water is gone, but it left
something behind: Ink Blight, a grey mould that eats ink and spreads from shelf to shelf. The
players are archivists. Together they must restore five damaged collections before the blight
creeps down the stairs into the Founders' Vault, where the oldest records of the city are kept.

Everyone wins together or everyone loses together. There is no individual winner.

## The five collections

The archive holds five collections, and each one needs a different set of folio cards to be
restored: the Maps, the Ledgers, the Star Charts, the Songbooks and the Charters. The Maps and
the Star Charts are kept in the North wing, the Ledgers in the East wing, the Songbooks in the
South wing and the Charters in the West wing. The Reading Hall sits in the middle and connects
all four wings.

## Components

- 1 archive board showing the four wings, the Reading Hall and the blight track (spaces 0 to 12)
- 4 archivist pawns and 4 role cards (Binder, Cartographer, Scribe, Conservator)
- 72 folio cards in four kinds: Vellum, Thread, Ink and Seal (18 of each kind)
- 24 blight tokens and 1 blight marker
- 15 archive event cards
- 5 restoration markers
- 1 Keeper's bell, which marks the first player

## Players and time

The Archivists of Vellmoor is played by two to four players aged ten and up. A game takes
about 45 to 60 minutes. New groups should read the setup and turn structure pages first and
keep the action summary at hand during the first game.
